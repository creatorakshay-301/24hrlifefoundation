OUR STORY

A foundation shaped by care and community.

24 Hr Life Foundation is a registered Public Trust and Society based in Digras, Yavatmal, Maharashtra. Established in November 2025, the foundation focuses on Education, Healthcare, Social Welfare, Environment, and Sports & Cultural Development.

We believe meaningful progress comes from understanding grassroots needs, respecting every individual's dignity, and working alongside community members and government initiatives to bring welfare schemes directly to underserved families.

OUR MISSION

To empower orphaned, rural, tribal, and economically weaker sections of society by ensuring access to quality education, affordable healthcare, skill-based livelihood training, and timely disaster relief.

OUR VISION

To build an inclusive, healthy, and self-reliant society where every child is educated, every woman is financially independent, and communities thrive in harmony with nature.

WHAT WE DO

Working across the needs that matter.

01

Education & Differently-Abled Support

Supporting urban, rural, tribal, and orphaned students from early childhood (Balwadi) through higher, medical, engineering, and vocational education. We also focus on providing hostel facilities and dedicated educational support for blind, deaf-mute, and intellectually disabled students.

02

Public Health & Awareness

Establishing charitable clinics and hospitals, organizing blood and eye donation drives, running de-addiction centers, supporting child immunization initiatives, and spreading awareness against substance abuse and superstitions.

03

Women Empowerment & Social Welfare

Conducting certified training programs in tailoring, weaving, handicrafts, cottage industries, and beauty care to help unemployed and destitute women achieve financial independence. We also assist in organizing community marriages for underprivileged girls and connecting marginalized families with state and central government welfare schemes.

04

Environment, Animal Care & Disaster Relief

Leading tree plantation and conservation drives, promoting animal husbandry and veterinary care, and providing immediate relief to communities affected by natural calamities.

05

Sports & Cultural Heritage

Setting up gymnasiums and coaching facilities for sports like cricket, kabaddi, football, volleyball, and athletics to nurture rural talent for national and international competitions, while promoting local folk arts and cultural unity.
