OUR STORY

A foundation shaped by care and action.

24 Hr Life Foundation is a registered Public Trust based in Digras, Yavatmal, Maharashtra. The Foundation's focus areas are Education, Health, Environment and Social Work.

We believe meaningful progress comes from understanding local needs, respecting people and working with community members and capable partners.

As our initiatives develop, this website will share verified information about activities, partnerships and outcomes.
